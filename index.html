Course Details

Learn about available courses, subjects, class structure, and student support.

Course Structure

Physics: We have detailed theory classes, and later we explain mathematical problems covering all topics. Questions are chosen from a variety of board and admission test problems.

Higher Math: All theories are covered elaborately, and all math types of a chapter are solved. Important techniques and shortcuts for MCQ and admission tests are also explained.

Exams: We conduct exams after completing each chapter and take problem-solving classes.

Lecture sheets: We provide lecture sheets with mathematical problems, advanced concepts, and conceptual questions.

Class duration: 1 hour 30min+ (18-16 days per month).

Batch Rules & Discipline

These Physics and Higher Math batches follow strict rules and regulations.

Specialized syllabus: Batches are conducted based on the syllabus of Dhaka College, BNMPC, BMARPC, Dhaka City College, and Dhanmondi Ideal.

Student discipline: Students must be ready to study under intense pressure and maintain discipline.

Attendance: Students have to join every class regularly.

Class notes: Students must make notes from every class on a regular basis.
